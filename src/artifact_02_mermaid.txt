    %% Lifecycle Flows
    Sponsor -->|"1. Init Vault & Deposit SOL/USDC"| InitVault
    InitVault -->|"Allocates"| PDA_State
    InitVault -->|"Locks Capital"| PDA_Vault

    Builder -->|"2. Submit Deliverable (IPFS CID)"| SubmitMilestone
    SubmitMilestone -->|"Mutates Milestone Status to PENDING"| PDA_State

    Evaluator -->|"3. Attest & Verify Milestone Proof"| ApproveMilestone
    ApproveMilestone -->|"State = APPROVED"| PDA_State

    Builder -->|"4. Execute Payout Claim"| ReleaseFunds
    ReleaseFunds -->|"Transfers Pro-Rata Funds"| PDA_Vault
    PDA_Vault -->|"Disburses SOL/USDC"| Builder

    Sponsor -.->|"If Expired / Inactive"| Clawback
    Clawback -.->|"Refunds Residual Balance"| PDA_Vault

    %% Off-chain Event Observability
    SolanaCore -.->|"Emits Anchor Events"| RPC
    RPC -->|"Subscribes via WebSocket (confirmed)"| WorkerService
    WorkerService -->|"Dispatches Transaction Confirmation"| ResendAPI
    WorkerService -->|"Broadcasts Milestone Milestone Release"| SlackDiscord
